About this book

The 12 Ps of Biblical Masculinity

Reclaiming Authentic Manhood Through Biblical Truth

When men reject God’s design, families and societies fall apart. It’s time to reclaim biblical manhood. What does it truly mean to be a man? In a world where masculinity is often distorted, ridiculed, or misunderstood, Eddie’s counsel helps men return to God’s timeless plan. Drawing directly from Scripture and shaped by years of pastoral leadership, he offers a clear, compelling vision of authentic manhood through twelve essential roles every man is called to fulfill. A biblical man, clearly stated, is one who reflects the heart and character of Christ. More than just a book about masculinity, this is a call for transformation. With prophetic clarity and pastoral wisdom, Eddie challenges men to reject passivity, compromise, and cultural confusion, and instead rise to become the men God created them to be. If you want to lead with courage, live with conviction, and leave a legacy that lasts and honors God, this book will challenge you, shape you, and call you to reach higher.
