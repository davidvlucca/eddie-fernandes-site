About this book

The 12 Ps of Biblical Masculinity

Reclaiming Authentic Manhood Through Biblical Truth

The 12 Ps of Biblical Masculinity calls men back to God's design in a world where masculinity is often distorted. Drawing from Scripture and decades of pastoral leadership, Dr. Fernandes presents twelve essential roles that define authentic, biblical manhood — challenging men to reject passivity and compromise, and to embody courage, conviction, purity, responsibility, faith, and peace. Written for husbands, fathers, leaders, pastors, mentors, and men's groups, it offers a clear biblical framework for identity, character, leadership, family, and legacy.
