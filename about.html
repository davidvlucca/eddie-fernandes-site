About

Born in Lisbon. Raised in
Johannesburg. Called to the world.

Eddie Fernandes co-founded Riverside International Church in Cascais in 1996 alongside his wife Marta. What began as a single congregation has grown into the Riverside Federation of Churches, with communities in Cascais, Lisbon, Porto, and Albufeira. Other churches planted out of Cascais did not keep the Riverside name, yet have blossomed in other cities of Portugal and in other countries.

He holds a Doctor of Ministry (D.Min.) and trained for twelve years at four Christian institutions across three continents in Portugal, South Africa, and the United States. He is a pastor, chaplain, evangelist, mentor of leaders, and global preacher — having carried the Gospel to more than 75 countries over four decades. Every year he ministers in three to five countries, mostly in Africa, where his heart has its home.

Married to Marta Isabel, a gifted and talented minister in her own right, since 1989, he is the father of three married daughters and the grandfather of eight grandchildren, whom he loves and loves to spoil.
