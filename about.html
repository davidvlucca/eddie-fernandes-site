About

Born in Lisbon. Raised in
Johannesburg. Called to the world.

Eddie Fernandes co-founded Riverside International Church in Cascais in 1996 alongside his wife Marta. What began as a single congregation has grown into the Riverside Federation of Churches, with communities in Cascais, Lisbon, Porto, and Albufeira.

He holds a Doctor of Ministry (D.Min.) and has been trained at four Christian institutions across Portugal, South Africa, and the United States. He is a pastor, chaplain, evangelist, mentor of leaders, and global preacher — having carried the Gospel to more than 75 countries over four decades.

Married to Marta Isabel since 1989, father of three daughters and grandfather of eight.

FAQ

Common questions.

How can I invite Eddie to speak at my event? ▾
You can use the contact form on this website to send a speaking request. Please include the event date, location, format, and expected audience. Eddie's team will respond within a few business days.
What languages does Eddie preach in? ▾
Eddie is fully fluent in English and Portuguese and can preach or teach in both languages.
Are mentoring sessions available online? ▾
Yes. Sessions are available both in-person (Cascais, Portugal) and online via video call. Schedule through the Mentoring page.
Where can I buy Eddie's books? ▾
All books are available on Amazon and, for some titles, on Clube de Autores. Visit the Books page for direct purchase links for each title.
How are donations used? ▾
Donations support Eddie's global preaching and teaching ministry, mission trips, church planting, and the production of new books and resources.
Is Riverside International Church open to visitors? ▾
Yes! Riverside International Church in Cascais welcomes visitors. Services are held in English and Portuguese. Contact us for service times and location.
